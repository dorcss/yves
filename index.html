The answer I carry inside me

My Dearest Yves,

There is something beautiful about finding a person whose presence makes the future feel less frightening. For me, that person is you. When I think about the years waiting ahead, I do not simply picture places I want to visit or dreams I want to achieve. I picture your hand in mine, your voice in our home, and your presence beside me as we discover what life has prepared for us.

So, my love, if the question is whether I am ready to walk into tomorrow with you, whether I want to share the responsibilities, the unexpected turns, the quiet evenings, the celebrations, and the years that will change us both, then my answer is yes. Not because I know every detail of the road, but because you are the person with whom I want to discover it.

Yves, I am not offering you a promise that I will always know the right words or that I will never have a difficult day. I am offering something more honest: a woman willing to keep learning how to love you well. I want to understand you beyond what you say aloud, respect the person you are becoming, and give our relationship the care it deserves.

I want us to create a home where neither of us has to pretend to be strong every minute. A place where we can speak openly, laugh until ordinary worries become smaller, admit when we are wrong, and find our way back to one another after disagreements. I want our love to be something we practise, not merely something we say.

I choose the real you, Yves. The man with his own thoughts, ambitions, imperfections, and dreams. I want to celebrate your victories without making them about me, encourage you when progress is slow, and remind you of your worth when a difficult season makes you question how far you have come.

And I hope you will allow me to grow, too. I want to remain Germaine, a woman with dreams of her own, while building something meaningful with you. May we become partners who make room for each other's ambitions and find joy in seeing one another succeed. Let our togetherness make our individual lives richer.

When we are young, we may measure love by how quickly our hearts beat. As the years pass, I hope we will also recognise it in the practical little things: remembering what matters to each other, checking in after a tiring day, sharing responsibilities, making time when life becomes busy, and being careful with each other's trust.

I want to meet the older versions of us. I want to hear the stories we will tell about our early days, smile at the things we once worried about, and look at you with the quiet satisfaction of a woman who helped build a life with the man she truly wanted. I want our history to carry evidence of kindness, courage, patience, and thousands of decisions to keep caring for one another.

I pray that God gives us wisdom when choices are difficult, humility when pride tries to speak first, and gratitude when blessings arrive in unexpected forms. May we never become so occupied with chasing a better future that we forget to appreciate the beautiful things already growing between us.

Yves, I cannot see every chapter waiting for us. But I know the name I want beside mine as those chapters unfold. I want the ordinary mornings, the plans that work, the plans that change, the celebrations shared with people we love, and the peaceful moments when simply being together is enough.

So here is my answer, written without hesitation: yes to you, yes to building with you, yes to learning beside you, and yes to a future in which we keep treating our love as something precious. I am giving you my sincere intention to stand beside you, nurture our bond, and do my part in the life we create together.

If someone asks me what I see when I look toward forever, I will not need a complicated explanation. I see a life we have built with care. I see two people who have changed over time but have not stopped making room for each other. And somewhere in that picture, I see you smiling at me, still my Yves.

My answer is YES, Yves. I choose you as my companion, my partner, and the man with whom I want to write the unwritten pages of my life. Let us begin our forever with honest hearts, steady hands, and love that grows through action.

With a heart that has chosen you,
Germaine ♡
